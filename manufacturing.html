Is my formula safe?
Brands
Plants see a summary first. Full details are shared only after both sides sign an NDA.
What does it cost brands?
Brands
Nothing. Brands search plants and post projects for free.
Can a small brand start here?
Brands
Yes. Filter by minimum order, and look for plants that offer shared runs, where several brands share one batch and each keeps its own label.
How do I know a plant is licensed?
Brands
Licences and certificates are checked against public registers where they exist and shown with their expiry dates. Plants show us their premises, and every batch comes with a lab report.
How do I list my plant?
Manufacturers
Share your licences, certificates, lines and minimum orders. We verify them and publish your profile.
What does it cost manufacturers?
Manufacturers
Yes. Joining, comparing, quoting and ordering are free during early access. WhatsApp automation is a paid add-on because every message has a cost. Any other paid plan will come later, with at least 60 days' notice.
Do I have to show my free capacity?
Manufacturers
Only if you want to. Plants that share their free weeks are matched to projects that fit them.
Does Indently make products?
General
No. Licensed manufacturers make them. Indently verifies plants and runs the project between brand and manufacturer.
Do you take engineering or machining work?
General
No. The manufacturing network is for consumer products.
