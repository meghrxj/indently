Built-in intelligence

The platform does the working out

Rules you set, applied to every order. You approve, Indently handles the rest.

Smart split

Need 40 barrels with 10 urgent? Indently sends the urgent part to the nearest seller with stock and the rest to the lowest delivered price.

Quote reader

Send a photo or PDF of any quote. It is read into the same delivered-price table as the rest.

Payment due-date tracker

Every invoice with its due date, flagged when a supplier falls under the 45-day MSME rule.

Supplier risk alerts

A seller who misses GST filings or lets a licence lapse is paused before your next order.

Auto-reorder

When stock in Tally reaches your reorder level, a request is drafted for you to approve.

Substitute finder

When your usual brand is out of stock, see equivalent grades that meet the same spec.

Collections assistant

Polite payment reminders on WhatsApp before and after the due date, in your name.

Live e-auctions

Buyers open a timed bidding event. Invited, verified sellers see their rank and the gap, and can improve their bid until the clock runs out. A late bid extends the time.

What is Indently?
General
A B2B network where verified businesses source, sell and get products manufactured. Buyers post what they need, suppliers quote the delivered price, and brands find licensed contract manufacturers. Every order is tracked from request to payment.
Does Indently sell the goods?
General
No. Suppliers and manufacturers sell to you directly, on their own invoice. Indently verifies them, runs the quote and order flow, and handles the paperwork.
Is it free?
General
Yes. Joining, comparing, quoting and ordering are free during early access. WhatsApp automation is a paid add-on because every message has a cost. Any other paid plan will come later, with at least 60 days' notice.
How do you verify businesses?
General
We check GST registration and filing history, bank details and Udyam where it applies. Sellers and plants also show us their premises by video or a site visit, and each profile says which. After that, every order adds to their scorecard.
Where is Indently available?
General
We are opening one industrial region at a time so that every request finds enough sellers. Join early access and tell us where you are.
What does delivered price mean?
Buyers
The price at your gate: the item, freight to your site and GST, per unit. Quotes are ranked on that number, so a low base price that turns expensive later never comes out on top.
What is smart split?
Buyers
If part of an order is urgent, Indently sends that part to the nearest seller with stock and the rest to the lowest delivered price. You approve once and two orders go out.
Can I quote on WhatsApp?
Sellers
Yes. Requests arrive on WhatsApp. Reply with your prices or a photo of your quote, and confirm what we read back before it is sent.
Is my formula safe?
Manufacturing
Plants see a summary first. Full details are shared only after both sides sign an NDA.
Can a small brand find a plant?
Manufacturing
Yes. Filter by minimum order, and look for plants that offer shared runs, where several brands share one batch and each keeps its own label.
