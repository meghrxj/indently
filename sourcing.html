What can I buy on Indently?
General
Maintenance, repair and operating supplies, consumables, packaging and the other items your business buys again and again. Bulk commodities are not our focus.
How are suppliers chosen for my request?
General
Each request goes to a handful of verified suppliers that stock the item, deliver to your site and have a good record. You can add your own suppliers to the same request.
Is it free for buyers?
Buyers
Yes. Joining, comparing, quoting and ordering are free during early access. WhatsApp automation is a paid add-on because every message has a cost. Any other paid plan will come later, with at least 60 days' notice.
How does smart split decide?
Buyers
You tell it which quantity is urgent and by when. It checks which verified sellers have stock and can reach you in time, then places the rest with the lowest delivered price. You see the plan and approve it before anything is ordered.
Can I add the suppliers I use today?
Buyers
Yes. Invite them and they quote next to new suppliers, with the same delivered-price view.
Can my whole team use it?
Buyers
Yes. Add buyers, approvers and accounts staff, each with their own role and approval limits.
Who can see my phone number?
Buyers
Nobody, until you choose a quote.
Who do I pay?
Payments
You pay the supplier on their invoice, on the terms you agree with them. With protected payment, a licensed partner holds the money until you confirm delivery.
Does it work with Tally?
Payments
Orders, receipts and supplier invoices export in a Tally-ready format, so your accounts team does not type them again. Connectors for Tally and Zoho Books keep both sides in sync.
